Remote Support

Only use this page if you are on the phone with FlossByte. We will never ask you to come to this page from an email or text you were not expecting.

Download the Support App

What happens next

  1. Click the button above, then open the file that downloads. It appears at the top right of your browser window. Downloading alone is not enough, the app has to be opened.
  2. If Windows asks whether to run it or allow it, click Run or Yes. Nothing gets installed on your computer.
  3. A small window opens with a 9-digit code. Read that code to us over the phone.
  4. Stay at the computer while we work. You can watch everything we do, and closing that window ends the session right away.
On a Mac? On a Mac, your computer may ask you to allow screen recording the first time. We will walk you through it.

This page is for a FlossByte technician connecting to this computer. If you need to reach your office computers from home, call us instead.
