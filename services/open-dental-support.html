Why Specialized IT Matters

Why Open Dental Needs Specialized IT

Open Dental is the leading open-source practice management software — trusted by thousands of dental offices for its flexibility, transparency, and powerful feature set. But that flexibility comes with a catch: Open Dental requires proper IT infrastructure to run reliably.

Unlike cloud-only platforms that handle everything behind the scenes, Open Dental runs on MySQL databases that need proper server configuration, performance tuning, and a robust backup strategy. The eConnector service must stay online for web forms, patient portal access, and automated features to function correctly. Without specialized IT, practices experience slow charting, failed backups, and eConnector crashes that disrupt daily operations.

FlossByte's Open Dental IT support is built by technicians who work with Open Dental every day. We understand the database architecture, the eConnector's requirements, the update process, and the real-world challenges of running Open Dental in a dental practice environment — whether on-premise, in the cloud, or in a hybrid setup.

Common Open Dental IT Problems

Slow charting and queries from an untuned MySQL database
eConnector crashes that break web forms and patient portal
