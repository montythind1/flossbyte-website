## Technical SEO Watchlist

- Title tags: many city and blog pages are above 65 characters. This is not automatically wrong, but Google may rewrite them. Prioritize shortening high-value pages first.
- Meta descriptions: several blog and region pages exceed 170 characters. Rewrite pages that already receive impressions in Google Search Console.
- Review schema: only keep AggregateRating markup where ratings/reviews are visible, verifiable, and compliant with Google review snippet guidelines.
- City pages: keep expanding unique local proof, neighborhoods served, software issues seen locally, and nearby internal links to avoid thin doorway-page risk.
- Google Business Profile: optimize service areas, services, Q&A, photos, and review acquisition because "near me" local pack visibility will not come from website changes alone.
