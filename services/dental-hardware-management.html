The Challenge

Why Dental Hardware Requires Specialized Management

Dental practices are not standard offices. Your server needs to handle Dentrix or Eaglesoft databases serving multiple operatories simultaneously. Your imaging workstations must render high-resolution panoramic X-rays, CBCT volumes, and intraoral scans without lag. Your front-desk stations must process insurance claims, patient check-ins, and payment transactions all day long without freezing or crashing.

Generic IT providers order the same business-class desktops they use for accounting firms and law offices. Those machines are not built to handle dental imaging software, practice management databases, or the USB and serial connections that intraoral cameras and digital sensors require. The result is slow image loading, database timeouts, and frustrated staff who lose productive chair time waiting on technology.

Without proactive lifecycle planning, hardware failures become emergencies. A server that crashes mid-morning means no access to patient records, no scheduling, no billing — and a waiting room full of patients. Replacing that server on an emergency basis costs significantly more and takes days longer than a planned refresh. FlossByte eliminates these surprises with structured hardware management that keeps your practice running predictably and efficiently.

The exact specifications depend on your practice size, the number of concurrent users accessing the system, and whether you are running Dentrix Ascend or Dentrix G7 on-premise.
