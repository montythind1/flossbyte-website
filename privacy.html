Cookies

Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies set by Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with our site. These cookies collect aggregated, anonymous usage data.

We do not use cookies for advertising or retargeting purposes. Our contact form (Formspree) does not set any cookies.

You can control cookies through your browser settings or use the Google Analytics Opt-Out Add-on. Disabling cookies will not affect the core functionality of our website.

HIPAA

Protected Health Information

As a dental IT services provider, FlossByte may access or handle protected health information (PHI) on behalf of our dental practice clients. When we do so, we act as a HIPAA Business Associate and are bound by the terms of a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with each client.

PHI handled under a BAA is governed by HIPAA regulations and the terms of the applicable BAA — not this Privacy Policy. This Privacy Policy applies only to information collected through our website and general business operations.

Important

We do not collect PHI through our website. Our contact form is not intended for the submission of patient health information. If you are a dental practice needing to share PHI, please contact us directly to establish a BAA first.

California Residents (CCPA)

The California Consumer Privacy Act provides additional rights:

Right to Know — request disclosure of data collected
Right to Delete — request deletion of your data

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy. Contact form submissions are retained for up to 24 months. Google Analytics data is retained according to our GA4 configuration settings.

Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.
