What Is a Dental vCIO?

A Technology Leader Who Speaks Dental

A virtual Chief Information Officer (vCIO) is a senior technology advisor who works with your practice on an ongoing basis — without being a full-time employee. Think of it as having a CIO-level strategist dedicated to your dental practice.

Most dental practices make technology decisions reactively: buying hardware when something breaks, choosing software because a sales rep called, or ignoring compliance until an audit looms. A dental vCIO flips that script — giving you a proactive technology roadmap aligned with your practice goals, budget, and growth plans.

Unlike generic IT consultants, FlossByte's vCIO service is built exclusively for dental. We understand Dentrix migrations, HIPAA audit preparation, imaging infrastructure, DSO technology standardization, and the real-world challenges of running a modern dental practice.
