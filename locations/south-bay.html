How We Cover the South Bay

One Base in San Jose, Three Corridors of Practices

FlossByte works out of San Jose, and South Bay support falls into a pattern set by the freeways. Most issues are handled remotely. When a job needs hands in the office, it is scheduled around the corridor the practice sits on. Server swaps, migrations, and other heavy work are staged during the week, then cut over after hours, after the last patient has gone home.

Highway 101: San Jose to Gilroy

The south run covers San Jose, Morgan Hill, and Gilroy. Practices at this end of the valley are often single-location family offices where the server sits in a back room and nobody has touched the network since it was installed. On-site visits are grouped along the corridor in one trip, and staged equipment rides with the tech instead of going by courier.

Interstate 880: Milpitas and North San Jose

The 880 corridor is plaza and business-park dentistry, including a lot of multi-operatory offices in Milpitas. The recurring work here is imaging: CBCT and sensor traffic moving across networks that were built for front-desk email. Visits are timed against commute peaks on 880 so an afternoon call does not turn into an evening in traffic.

Highway 85: The West Side

Cupertino, Campbell, Saratoga, Los Gatos, and Los Altos sit along 85 and 17. Older buildings and converted houses are common on this side, which means the survey before any install matters: panel capacity, old cabling, and where a rack can actually go. Replacement hardware runs on a planned cycle here, swapped after hours a piece at a time.

The setup follows the office, not the corridor. A practice running Dentrix, Eaglesoft, or Open Dental on a server in the building gets the Sophos firewall profile. A practice on cloud software gets UniFi. Pricing is published and flat either way, with managed IT from $59 per device per month.
