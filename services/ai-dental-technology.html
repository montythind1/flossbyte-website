HIPAA & AI

The Only Dental IT Partner That Speaks Both AI and HIPAA

AI tools process patient data — X-rays, medical histories, appointment records. That makes them subject to HIPAA. Most AI vendors won't tell you whether their product is truly compliant. FlossByte does the due diligence so you don't have to.

Our AI Compliance Checklist

BAA Verification — Does the AI vendor sign a Business Associate Agreement?
Data Encryption — Is patient data encrypted in transit and at rest?
Data Residency — Where is patient data processed and stored?
Access Controls — Who can view the AI-processed results?
Audit Logging — Is there a trail of AI-generated recommendations?
