Dental server problems rarely begin as a dramatic outage.

They usually start as small interruptions that the team works around:

- Dentrix or Eaglesoft takes longer to open
- Imaging files load slowly in multiple rooms

- Mapped drives disappear from workstations
- The server is out of warranty, low on storage, or undocumented
