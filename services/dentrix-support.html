Common Dentrix Issues We Solve

Dentrix is one of the most widely used practice management systems in dentistry — but it demands properly configured infrastructure to perform well. When something goes wrong, your entire practice slows down.

Most Dentrix performance problems aren't caused by the software itself. They stem from inadequate server hardware, misconfigured networks, outdated SQL databases, or improperly set permissions. Generic IT providers often lack the dental-specific knowledge to diagnose these issues quickly.

FlossByte specializes in dental IT. We understand Dentrix server requirements, Henry Schein's recommended configurations, and the real-world infrastructure challenges that cause Dentrix to underperform. Whether your system is sluggish, crashing, or needs a full upgrade, we know exactly where to look.
