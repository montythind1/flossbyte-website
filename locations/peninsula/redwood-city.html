Redwood City Practice Notes

Imaging-Heavy Practices Need a Different Network

Redwood City is the county seat, and its dental map follows its medical map: practices cluster around Sequoia Hospital and the Kaiser campus, and along El Camino Real and Woodside Road. These tend to be bigger offices than the Peninsula average, with more operatories, specialists under one roof, and CBCT and panoramic imaging moving very large files around the building all day long.

That changes the IT math. A CBCT scan that takes minutes to open is chair time lost, multiplied by every operatory waiting on it. So for Redwood City offices we build the imaging path first: wired high-speed runs to the server room, the imaging database on properly sized storage, and clinical traffic kept off the guest network segment. On-prem setups run behind Sophos; cloud imaging viewers and hosted practice management run over managed UniFi. When the practice adds chairs, the architecture already has room for them.

It is the same engineering behind our managed dental IT services and our Peninsula and SF coverage, sized for the practices that push the most data.
