Why It Matters

Imaging Problems Are Often IT Problems

When a sensor, CBCT, or imaging bridge fails, the issue might not be the device itself. It may be a workstation, driver, network path, storage permission, firewall rule, mapped drive, database bridge, or vendor account problem.

That is why dental imaging support needs both vendor coordination and IT ownership. FlossByte helps identify what belongs to the vendor, what belongs to the network, and what needs to be fixed inside the practice environment.
