At the Edge of the Metro

Supporting Livermore from Across the Valley

Livermore sits at the eastern end of the Tri-Valley, and that geography shapes how we support it. Our team is based in San Jose, so Livermore on-site work is planned deliberately rather than improvised: visits are scheduled as part of a regular East Bay run up I-680 and across I-580, the truck carries the parts the visit is likely to need, and anything that can be resolved remotely is resolved remotely before anyone drives anywhere.

The practice mix here is just as distinct. Long-established offices near First Street and along East Avenue often run on-premise Dentrix or Eaglesoft servers that have sat in the closet for years; those get a Sophos firewall profile, monitored and tested backups, and a written refresh plan instead of a surprise failure. Newer offices opening on the east side, around the Isabel Avenue corridor and the neighborhoods still being built out past Vasco Road, serve a patient base heavy with Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory and Sandia commuters, and many of them start cloud-based on Open Dental with a UniFi network and no server at all.

Either way, the standard is the one we hold in San Jose: cutovers and major upgrades after hours, HIPAA documentation an auditor can follow, and one flat plan starting at $59 per device per month. See everything included on our managed IT services page.
