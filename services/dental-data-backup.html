What's Included

Complete Dental Backup Protection

Our dental practice backup solution covers every layer of protection your practice needs — from automated daily backups to documented disaster recovery procedures and regular restore testing.

Automated Cloud Backup

Your patient data is automatically backed up to HIPAA-compliant, AES-256 encrypted cloud storage every day. No manual steps, no forgotten backups, no unprotected data. Backups run silently in the background without impacting practice operations or slowing down your dental software.

Local + Offsite Redundancy

We implement the industry-standard 3-2-1 backup strategy: three copies of your data, stored on two different media types, with one copy offsite. This protects against every scenario — from a single hard drive failure to a fire or flood that destroys your entire office.

Dental Software Backup

Purpose-built backup configurations for Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, and other dental practice management systems. We back up your databases, patient images, X-rays, documents, and application settings — not just generic file-level copies that miss critical dental data.

Disaster Recovery Planning

We create a documented disaster recovery plan tailored to your practice. It defines exactly who does what, in what order, and with what resources when disaster strikes. Your team will know the steps to follow whether you're dealing with ransomware, a server crash, or a natural disaster.

Rapid Recovery & Restoration

When the worst happens, speed matters. Our dental disaster recovery process is designed to get your practice back online as fast as possible — restoring your databases, patient records, images, and configurations with minimal downtime. Most recoveries are completed within hours, not days.
