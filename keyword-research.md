## 9. TECHNICAL SEO RECOMMENDATIONS FOR KEYWORD TARGETING

1. **Schema markup:** Add Service schema to all service pages with `areaServed` for Bay Area cities. Add FAQ schema to blog posts targeting question-based keywords.
2. **Internal linking:** Every blog post should link to relevant service pages and location pages. City pages should link to relevant blog posts.
3. **Title tag optimization:** Ensure every page's title tag includes the primary target keyword + location where applicable (e.g., "Dental IT Support San Jose | FlossByte").
4. **Google Business Profile:** Optimize for "dental IT support near me" -- ensure primary category is "Computer Support and Services" with "IT services" as secondary. Add all Bay Area service areas.
5. **Content freshness:** Update existing blog posts with 2026 statistics (ransomware increase, HIPAA fines, breach data) to maintain relevance.
