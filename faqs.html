HIPAA & Security
Yes. All FlossByte services are designed around HIPAA requirements. We sign Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with all of our clients, implement the technical safeguards HIPAA requires, and provide documentation to support your compliance program. We also offer a dedicated HIPAA hardening add-on that includes a full risk assessment, policy development, and employee training.
Absolutely. As a Business Associate under HIPAA, FlossByte is required to sign a BAA before accessing any Protected Health Information (PHI). We make this a standard part of our onboarding process for every dental client.
FlossByte clients with our HIPAA compliance package have a documented Incident Response Plan ready to activate. We will work immediately to contain the breach, assess the scope of exposure, notify affected parties according to HIPAA breach notification rules, and coordinate with your legal counsel. We also provide data recovery from our maintained backups where applicable.
