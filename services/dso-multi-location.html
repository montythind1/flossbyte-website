Why DSOs Need Specialized IT

The Multi-Location Technology Gap

Most dental IT providers are built to support a single office. When you're managing five, fifteen, or fifty locations, the rules change completely. Generic IT support doesn't scale — and the cracks show fast.

Every acquisition brings a different practice management system, a different network setup, different hardware generations, and different security postures. Without a deliberate technology strategy, your organization ends up with a patchwork of disconnected systems that drain staff productivity, create compliance blind spots, and make centralized reporting nearly impossible.

FlossByte specializes in dental IT at scale. We've helped Bay Area DSOs and multi-location groups build standardized, secure, and manageable technology environments — so leadership gets the visibility they need and every location runs on infrastructure that meets the same high standard.

DSO & Multi-Location IT Services

End-to-end technology management designed for dental organizations that operate across multiple sites and need consistency, security, and scalability at every location.

Multi-Site Network Architecture

Standardized network infrastructure across every location — same firewall policies, VLAN segmentation, wireless standards, and site-to-site VPN connectivity. Each office runs on a proven blueprint so new sites deploy faster and troubleshooting is consistent regardless of location.

M&A Technology Integration

When you acquire a new practice, we handle the full technology onboarding — network assessment, system consolidation, data migration, user provisioning, and security hardening. Our structured integration playbook gets new acquisitions onto your standard platform in weeks, not months.

Centralized HIPAA Compliance

Unified HIPAA policies, workforce training programs, and risk assessments applied consistently across all locations. We conduct regular audits at every site, maintain centralized documentation, and ensure your entire organization meets the same compliance standard — not just your flagship office.

Standardized Technology Stack

One approved hardware catalog, one set of software standards, and centralized vendor management. We define and enforce your technology standards so every location runs the same configurations — reducing support complexity, simplifying training, and giving you volume purchasing leverage.

Centralized Monitoring & Support

A single pane of glass for your entire organization. We monitor every server, workstation, firewall, and backup across all locations from one centralized dashboard. Your leadership team gets real-time visibility into IT health organization-wide, and our dental-fluent help desk supports every site.

Growth & Expansion Planning

A technology roadmap that aligns with your acquisition and expansion goals. We plan infrastructure for new locations before you sign the LOI, budget capital expenditures across your portfolio, and ensure your IT scales proactively — so technology never becomes a bottleneck for growth.
