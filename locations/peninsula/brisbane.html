Brisbane Practice Notes

Small City, Long Reach: Running IT From Visitacion Avenue

Brisbane has one main street, Visitacion Avenue, but a practice on it rarely serves Brisbane alone. Patients come over San Bruno Mountain from South San Francisco and Daly City, and the office fills its book like a much bigger city’s practice. Phones, reminders, online forms, and the patient portal all have to work at that regional scale, even when the team running them is three people.

The weak spot in offices like these is usually the plumbing: one internet connection and one server in a back room carrying everything. For Brisbane practices we harden both. The on-prem Dentrix or Eaglesoft server sits behind a Sophos firewall, clinical traffic runs on a managed UniFi network separated from the waiting-room WiFi, and backups leave the building encrypted. If that single server is the practice’s memory, and in a small office it is, then it gets protected like one.

It is the same approach we take across our Peninsula and SF coverage, delivered through managed dental IT services that a small team can actually run day to day.
