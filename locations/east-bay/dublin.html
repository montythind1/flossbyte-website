How a Dublin Buildout Gets Sequenced

Drops before drywall
Cat6 runs to every operatory, imaging bay, and front desk position while the walls are still open. Adding a drop after paint costs several times more.
Server or cloud, decided early
On-premise Dentrix runs behind a Sophos firewall. Cloud-based practices run on UniFi. That single decision shapes the closet layout, the switch count, and the budget.
Cutover after hours
Final configuration, imaging integration, and testing happen outside patient hours, so opening week starts with systems that already work.
One plan from day one
Monitoring, tested backups, and HIPAA safeguards are live before the first patient arrives, at $59 per device per month.

That sequencing is the difference between an office that opens on schedule and one that opens with a consumer router under the front desk. Buildouts run under the same managed plan as our established offices, and you can see exactly what that includes on our dental IT services page.
