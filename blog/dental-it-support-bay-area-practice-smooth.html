  • Daily encrypted cloud backups — Your patient data is backed up every day with end-to-end encryption, ensuring recoverability in any scenario
  • Email and communication encryption — All electronic communications containing PHI are encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) — We implement MFA across all practice systems to prevent unauthorized access
  • Real-time antivirus and firewall protection — Enterprise-grade security monitoring that catches threats before they reach your data
  • Ongoing compliance assessments — Regular reviews to ensure your practice stays compliant as regulations evolve

💡 Key takeaway: HIPAA compliance requires continuous attention, not a one-time setup. FlossByte handles the ongoing technical requirements so you can focus on patient care.
