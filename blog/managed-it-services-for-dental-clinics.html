What Are Managed IT Services for Dental Clinics?

Managed IT services are outsourced technology services where a dedicated provider takes responsibility for your practice's entire IT environment. For dental clinics, this means a partner who understands and manages:

  • Dental practice management software: Dentrix, Open Dental, Eaglesoft, and other dental-specific platforms.
  • Digital imaging systems: X-ray equipment, intraoral cameras, and CBCT integration with your network.
  • HIPAA compliance: Security configurations, risk assessments, encrypted communications, and documentation.
  • Data backups: Automated daily backups with tested recovery procedures for dental databases.
  • Proactive monitoring: 24/7 monitoring of your systems to catch and resolve issues before they cause downtime.
